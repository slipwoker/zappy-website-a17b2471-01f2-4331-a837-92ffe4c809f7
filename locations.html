Easy Parking & Access

Our warehouse at 150 Madison Street, Oneida offers ample free parking right at the entrance. The store is single-level with wide aisles, making it accessible for strollers, wheelchairs, and carts for your bulk candy hauls. Look for the bright candy-striped awning!

Bulk Ordering Made Simple

Need candy for a big event? Our friendly staff is trained to help you estimate quantities, mix and match varieties, and even pre-pack your order for pickup. Just ask at the counter or call ahead — we'll have your sweets ready when you arrive.

Party & Gift Inspiration

From birthday buffets to wedding candy bars, Center Candy Warehouse is your go-to for themed confectionery. Explore our seasonal displays, custom gift baskets, and colorful packaging options. Not sure where to start? Our team loves helping you create something unforgettable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Center Candy Warehouse — from store visits to bulk orders and gift ideas.

What are your store hours?

Our Oneida location at 150 Madison Street is open Monday through Saturday from 10 AM to 7 PM, and Sunday from 12 PM to 5 PM. Hours may vary on holidays — give us a call before heading over.

Do you offer bulk candy?

Absolutely! Bulk candy is our specialty. Visit our warehouse to pick your own mix from hundreds of bins, or contact us for pre-packed bulk orders perfect for parties, events, and corporate gifting.

Can I create a custom gift basket?

Yes! We love helping you build the perfect candy gift. Choose your favorite treats and we'll arrange them in a beautiful basket or gift box. Stop by the store or reach out to start designing your custom gift.

Where are you located?

We're at 150 Madison Street in Oneida, New York. You'll find us right in the heart of the city with easy parking. Looking for confectionery retailers in the area? We're your destination for albanese confectionery and more.

Do you carry specialty or retro candy?

Yes! We stock a wide range of nostalgic and hard-to-find candies alongside today's favorites. If you're hunting for squirrel nuts candy retailers or a specific retro treat, chances are we have it — or we can track it down.

How do I place an order?
